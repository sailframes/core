Resolved sea-breeze gusts over the race course from the 111 m large-eddy nest (d05, 1-min). This shows the expected gust structure — puff magnitude, cell size, oscillation, and which side is gustier — through the race window.
⚠ Expected structure, not a per-puff clock. LES gives a physically-plausible realization of the turbulence, not the exact timing of any single puff (that's chaotic). Read the character — how big the puffs are, how often, which side — not "a puff at 13:14:32". Real-time "happening now" still needs live obs (radar + on-boat wind).

Observed sea-breeze front — radar & satellite

Independent obs at the event moment (yellow-zone fill, ~13:10 ET). These corroborate the modeled gusts: KBOX shows a clear-air fine line and GOES-19 shows a cumulus line — both crossing the course, with clear/marine air to the SE (the sea-breeze side).
Note: KBOX is ~64 km away, so its 0.5° beam grazes the top of a shallow front — the fine line is real but marginal/broken at this range (scattered enhanced echo, not a razor line). The time-lapse shows it drifting onshore.
